# HeyGen script: "Your first day on 1325.AI" (new business owner walkthrough)

## My take
Good idea. One long video covering everything would run 15+ minutes, and most owners stop watching after about 3. I recommend:
- **One main video (about 6–7 minutes)** that follows one made-up owner, "Denise," who runs a beauty salon, from sign-up through every main tool.
- **Chapter markers**, so you can also cut it into short 45–60 second clips for social media and the help center.

## What the script will cover (in order)
1. **Hook (0:00–0:20):** Denise has no time and no staff. Today, 42 Agentic AI Employees go to work for her.
2. **Sign up:** create a free account at 1325.ai and choose "Business."
3. **Claim or add the listing:** find her existing listing and claim it, or add a new one (name, category, address, phone, hours).
4. **Business profile:** photos, logo, description, website. Mentions the new 20 MB photo limit, so iPhone photos upload fine.
5. **Verification:** upload documents to get the verified badge.
6. **Meet Kayla and the 42 Agentic AI Employees:** what they do day and night; "~4 Roles Covered" and $18,000+/mo in savings.
7. **Bookings:** turn on "Book Appointment," set services and times, see new bookings come in.
8. **QR code and loyalty:** print the QR code, customers scan at checkout, earn points, return.
9. **Products and services:** add items with photos.
10. **Dashboard and analytics:** views, scans, customers, revenue.
11. **Finances and records:** payments and saved documents.
12. **Get found by AI assistants:** her business appears when people ask AI tools for recommendations.
13. **Grow (optional upgrades):** featured placement, multiple locations, B2B connections, and the Holiday Special (Pro $149/mo, locked in forever, through Dec 31).
14. **Close:** "Sign up free at 1325.AI. The future is already here."

## Format you'll get
- Scene-by-scene lines: **what's on screen** (screen recording or B-roll) next to **what the presenter says**.
- Time stamps per scene.
- "Thirteen twenty-five A-I" spelled out in the spoken lines only, so HeyGen says it correctly.
- A short HeyGen prompt block at the top (tone, avatar style, navy and gold look).
- Saved as a document in your Files, plus pasted here in chat so you can copy it.

## Before I check each step
I'll open each business-owner page in the app so every step in the script matches what owners actually see. No changes to the website.
