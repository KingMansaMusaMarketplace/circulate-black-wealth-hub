Resolution in Memoriam

Craig L. Stevenson

Co-Founder, 1325.AI  ·  February 3, 1962 — August 7, 2026

WHEREAS, Craig L. Stevenson stood among the founders of 1325.AI, lending his name, his labor, and his belief to an idea long before that idea had proof, funding, or applause; and

WHEREAS, he understood that a dollar circulated within our own community is not merely a transaction but an act of self-determination, and he gave himself to the work of building the rails on which that circulation could travel; and

WHEREAS, he carried the founding vision of 1325.AI — that Black-owned businesses across this nation and across the world deserve to be found, to be trusted, and to be prosperous — and he carried it with patience in the lean seasons and with grace in the bright ones; and

WHEREAS, he counseled his partners honestly, encouraged the discouraged, opened doors he did not need to open, and treated every business owner who came to this company as a neighbor rather than a customer; and

WHEREAS, the platform he helped found now serves tens of thousands of verified businesses and the families, employees, and communities who depend upon them, so that his labor continues in work he will not see completed; and

WHEREAS, his passing on Friday, August 7, 2026, leaves this company, this board, and this community with a loss that no successor can fill and no record can measure; and

WHEREAS, those who build for generations they will never meet deserve to be remembered by the generations they served;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Directors of 1325.AI does hereby honor the life, character, and enduring contribution of our Co-Founder, Craig L. Stevenson, and does record its profound gratitude for the years he gave to this mission.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the title of Co-Founder shall be permanently affixed to his name in every record, roster, and public presentation of this company, in perpetuity.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this Board extends its deepest sympathy to his family, and pledges to them that the company he helped found will stand as a living memorial to his work.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this Resolution be spread upon the permanent minutes of 1325.AI, that a copy be presented to the family of Craig L. Stevenson, and that a copy be read aloud at his homegoing service.

Homegoing Service
Thursday, August 27, 2026

“Well done, good and faithful servant.”

Adopted this 16th day of August, in the year Two Thousand Twenty-Six,
by the Board of Directors of 1325.AI, Chicago, Illinois.
