1325 Clinical — health.1325.ai

1325 Clinical is the healthcare enterprise division of 1325.AI. It is an AI-native electronic medical records (EMR) platform for physician practices, live as an investor demo at health.1325.ai. This page describes the health platform only. It is separate from the 1325.AI Black-owned business directory at 1325.ai.

Important status: 1325 Clinical currently uses fictional sample patients only. No real patient records are stored. The platform is not yet HIPAA compliant. Real records require dedicated healthcare hosting (AWS or Microsoft) with signed Business Associate Agreements (BAAs). Connections to Epic, Oracle Health (Cerner), labs and pharmacies are planned, not live.

One-sentence pitch

health.1325.ai is an autonomous AI clinical operating system: it combines clinic charting, automated patient phone outreach, and 4,000-page record review for compulsory medical examinations (CME) and legal-medical cases into a single, Black-owned platform.

Kayla, Ph.D. — Clinical AI Co-Pilot

Kayla is the AI built into every screen. On health.1325.ai she answers as a doctoral-level expert in medical record systems: charting, billing codes, prior authorization and appeals, record review and remote monitoring. Doctors can type or speak and she answers out loud. Kayla organizes records and drafts work; the doctor makes every medical decision. Kayla does not diagnose or prescribe.

Where it fits in the market

  • Legacy clinic systems (athenahealth, eClinicalWorks, Epic, Oracle Cerner) hold charts and billing but were built as digital filing cabinets. Kayla does the busywork instead.
  • New AI point tools (Attune.ai for calls, Abridge and Ambience for notes, Helio RCM and SmarterDx for billing) each solve one job. 1325 Clinical puts them in one place under Kayla.
  • Legal-medical record review (Wisedocs, ChartRequest) serves CME and injury cases. Standard EMRs can't turn 4,000 scanned pages into a timeline; the CME Record Review screen is built for this.
